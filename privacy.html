Expenses – Privacy policy

This app is a private tool, used only by its owner to track their own expenses.

It reads the owner's own Gmail, Google Drive and Google Sheets to find payment slips, invoices and bank statements, and writes the results to the owner's own Google Sheets and Drive.

No data is shared with anyone, sold, or stored outside the owner's Google account. The app has no other users.
